## Music screening and preparation / 配乐筛查与制作

The [screening report](../../docs/audio-music-screening-2026-10-28.json) records PANNs Cnn6 Speech/Music class scores for every non-overlapping 10-second interval of both final MP3s, plus a piano control and the previous rejected mixed interview. The model checkpoint’s MD5 matches its publisher’s record. Both final recordings have low Music scores throughout; the old mixed interview has strong Music activations in several intervals. Scores are an automated screening aid, not a calibrated probability or a guarantee that all faint music is absent. No independent human listening rating is claimed.

[配乐筛查报告](../../docs/audio-music-screening-2026-10-28.json) 保存两段最终 MP3 每 10 秒的 PANNs Cnn6 语音／音乐得分，并保留钢琴对照和上一版混有配乐的采访对照。模型 MD5 与发布者记录一致；最终两段的音乐得分持续较低，旧成片在多段有显著音乐响应。模型得分仅用于筛查，不是校准概率，也不能保证检出所有微弱配乐；这里不声称完成独立人工听评。

Prepared on 2026-10-28 with [`scripts/prepare-audio-excerpts.py`](../../scripts/prepare-audio-excerpts.py). Each speech clip is one continuous source interval, with no sentence concatenation, voice synthesis or music-removal processing. Decode timestamps are normalized before trimming to handle irregular timestamps in the archival Ogg file. Timestamps reset after trimming; 15 ms edge fades avoid cut clicks. Speech is 44.1 kHz mono MP3 at 96 kbps; music remains 128 kbps stereo. Speed and pitch are unchanged.

于 2026-10-28 使用上述脚本制作。两段语音均保留一个连续原声区间，未拼接短句、合成声音或做伴奏分离。先按解码样本重建时间戳，以兼容历史 Ogg 的不规则时间戳，再截取并重置为片段时间轴；两端各作 15 ms 淡入淡出。语音采用 44.1 kHz、96 kbps 单声道 MP3，音乐保持 128 kbps 双声道，速度与音高不变。

For reproduction, download the four originals above as `chopin.ogg`, `vivaldi.ogg`, `kennedy.ogg` and `zheng-raw.webm`, then run:

复现时，将以上原素材分别命名为上述四个文件，并执行：

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare-audio-excerpts.py /path/to/original-recordings
npm test
```

With FFmpeg installed, the signal regression test decodes all four files and checks actual 60-second duration, bounded opening/trailing quiet and sound in every 10-second interval. It explicitly skips without FFmpeg; asset changes require running it with FFmpeg installed. Signal/energy checks alone do not establish that a recording has no music. Each new speech clip has a new ID and URL; historical published assets remain available. Practice continues to use the Chopin recording.

安装 FFmpeg 后，信号回归测试解码四段文件，检查实际 60 秒时长、首尾空白和每 10 秒的有效声音；缺少 FFmpeg 时明确跳过，更改素材时应安装后执行。仅凭能量检查不能判定无配乐。新语音使用新 ID 和 URL；已发布的历史素材保留，练习继续使用肖邦录音。
